# Sales Performance Analysis Report

---

## 1. Executive Summary

During the evaluated 8-month period (January 2025 – August 2025), the business generated **1,373,850.0** in total net revenue across **20 total orders**, selling a total quantity of **157 items**. Revenue generation is heavily concentrated in the **Electronics** category (78.89% of net sales) and heavily driven by two regions: **North** (40.44%) and **West** (38.46%). 

While the Average Order Value (AOV) is strong at **68,692.5**, the business faces critical operational risks: **100% of the customer base consists of one-time buyers** (20 unique customers across 20 orders), and net revenue has experienced a continuous downward trajectory over the last two months (July and August 2025).

---

## 11. Business Risks

1. **Zero Customer Retention:** With 20 orders from 20 unique customers, there is no recurring revenue model. Acquisition costs could severely eat into margins if repeat orders are not established.
2. **Category Concentration Risk:** Electronics accounts for 78.89% of revenue. Any supply chain disruption or demand decrease in this category puts the majority of company revenue at risk.
3. **Recent Revenue Decline:** Revenue dropped by -33.66% in July and a further -18.0% in August, alongside a drop in monthly order volume from 3 to 2 orders per month.
4. **Regional Revenue Imbalance:** East and South regions contribute only 6.99% and 14.12% of total revenue respectively, despite generating similar or higher order counts compared to North and West.

---

## 12. Three Actionable Recommendations

1. **Launch a Re-engagement & Loyalty Program:** 
   * Reach out to all 20 existing high-value customers (such as Akash Verma and Ravi Kumar) with tailored post-purchase promotions or hardware maintenance plans to convert one-time buyers into recurring accounts.
2. **Cross-Sell High-Value Electronics into the East & South Regions:** 
   * The East region has strong order activity and high volume demand (95 units sold), but low revenue (96,030.0). Introduce targeted cross-selling strategies to bundle high-margin Electronics (Laptops, Monitors) alongside basic Office Supplies in these regions.
3. **Bundle Low-Performing Products with Top Sellers:** 
   * Pair slower-moving items like Printers (56,520.0 revenue) and Printer Paper with top-performing Laptops and Monitors as discounted hardware/office bundles. This will raise the average deal size and clear lower-performing product inventory without diluting margins.
